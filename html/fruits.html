Fruits

Apple

Apple is a sweet and crunchy fruit that is commonly eaten fresh all over the world. It comes in many varieties such as red, green, and yellow, each having a slightly different taste and texture. Apples are rich in dietary fiber and antioxidants, which help in maintaining good digestion. They are often used in juices, pies, salads, and desserts because of their pleasant flavor. Eating an apple regularly is often associated with a healthy lifestyle.

Banana

Banana is a soft and creamy fruit with a naturally sweet taste that is easy to eat. It is a great source of quick energy and is especially popular among athletes. Bananas contain important nutrients like potassium, vitamin B6, and magnesium. They are commonly eaten raw but are also used in milkshakes, cakes, and breakfast cereals. Because of their convenience, bananas are one of the most consumed fruits in the world.

Orange

Orange is a citrus fruit known for its refreshing juice and bright color. It has a tangy and slightly sweet flavor that many people enjoy. Oranges are an excellent source of vitamin C, which helps strengthen the immune system. They are widely used to make juices, marmalades, and flavored desserts. Consuming oranges regularly can help keep the body hydrated and healthy.

Mango

Mango is a tropical fruit famous for its rich taste and juicy texture. It is often called the king of fruits in many parts of the world. Mangoes are packed with vitamins like A and C, which are good for skin and eyesight. They are used in smoothies, ice creams, pickles, and traditional sweets. During the summer season, mangoes are especially popular and widely enjoyed.
